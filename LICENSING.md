# Licensing status

No project-wide open-source licence is currently granted for this repository.
In the absence of an explicit licence, the normal copyright restrictions
apply. Do not assume that public source availability grants permission to copy,
redistribute, sublicense or publish derived binaries.

The repository contains original integration work, generated patch material
and work derived from several upstream projects. Those components retain their
respective ownership and licence conditions. The ElkWiFi revision used as the
host-ROM basis does not contain a licence file, so its redistribution terms
must be clarified before a general source or binary release.

Third-party provenance and known licence information are recorded in
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and in notices retained beside vendored source.
Raspberry Pi and Broadcom firmware in generated hardware-test bundles retains
its accompanying licence file.

Contributors must submit only material they have the right to contribute and
must identify copied or derived material in the pull request. Accepting a
contribution does not change the licensing status described here.
