# Code of conduct

## Expected conduct

Project participants must:

- discuss technical disagreements with respect and specific evidence;
- distinguish confirmed results from assumptions and incomplete tests;
- accept that hardware reports may contradict emulator results;
- avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination and deliberate disruption;
- protect credentials, private keys, network captures and other sensitive data;
- credit upstream authors and contributors accurately.

Direct criticism of code, tests and engineering decisions is welcome. Abuse of
the people involved is not.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release notes and project-related communication in other public or private
channels.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not publish a conduct report as a repository issue. Report it privately to
the maintainer using the contact details on the
[`peteclarke-del`](https://github.com/peteclarke-del) GitHub profile. Include
links or copies of the relevant material and explain the requested outcome.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, reject
contributions, restrict participation or refer a matter to the operator of the
affected service. Reports will be handled as privately as the circumstances
allow. A maintainer who is directly involved in a report must seek an impartial
third party before taking irreversible action.
